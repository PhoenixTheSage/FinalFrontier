# Hipparcos derived data

Credit: ESA

ESA, 1997, The Hipparcos and Tycho Catalogues, ESA SP-1200.

Hipparcos.bin, Hipparcos.csv, Tools/Catalogue/Hipparcos.tsv and the catalogue metadata are derived from the
Hipparcos Main Catalogue (CDS I/239). These data are distributed under
[CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/igo/),
including its attribution and noncommercial conditions, separately from the
plugin source-code license. No ESA endorsement is implied.

[ESA catalogue and licensing statement](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/hipparcos/catalogues)
[CDS field documentation](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/cats/I/239/ReadMe)

Changes: filtered to Johnson V magnitude <= 8 with available positions;
converted fixed ICRS J1991.25 coordinates to unit vectors; converted magnitudes
to relative flux; assigned an artistic linear RGB tint from B-V; sorted into
a spatial index. Proper motion, variability and separate double-star component
solutions are not simulated. Missing B-V uses a neutral solar-like value.
No nebula or Milky Way imagery is included.
